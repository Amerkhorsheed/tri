Load the tri-method skill first. Open tri/DECISIONS.md. If $ARGUMENTS states a resolved choice by the owner: append the next D- entry (decision + rationale). Entries are append-only — supersede, never rewrite. Confirm the entry text with the user before writing if the wording is ambiguous.
