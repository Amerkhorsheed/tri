Load the tri-method skill first. Open tri/DECISIONS.md. If $ARGUMENTS states a resolved choice by the owner: append the next D- entry (decision + rationale). If it states an open question nobody at the keyboard is authorized to answer: append the next B- entry (question, owner, which tasks it blocks) and set those tasks Blocked. Entries are append-only — supersede, never rewrite. If the classification or wording is ambiguous, put it to the user with AskUserQuestion (e.g. resolved D- entry vs open B- entry, or which of two readings to record) rather than guessing or asking in prose.
